How the lookup is built, and what holds it

On 2026.9.7 an agent started by another through sessions_spawn keeps only the tools its asker is allowed. The docs speak of "inherited tool policy", and the installed code shows the mechanism: the asker's list of allowed tools and its denies are stored on the helper's session and applied as the last filter, also when the helper is a different agent with a tool list of its own. So the catalog's first design, in which your agent denies the Gmail tools and the reader it starts still reads mail, cannot work. On 13 Oct you chose the shape that can ("go with the first version") and said of the helpers still to come: "Not just email reader, we expect to adopt this pattern (Happy main agent hold tools, then dispatch sub-agent to narrow tool selection to do their specific work)".

For mail that is three things. Your own agent, tony-main, is allowed the reader's three Gmail read tools and the hand-off. Its rules tell it to hand every mail question to tony-mail and never to call those tools itself. And the reader's own tool list is what narrows the hand-off: of everything your agent holds, the reader keeps the three Gmail tools and nothing else. Mail is then read in the reader's session, and only its short answer comes back into your chat.

What this changes: until H1 a tool limit keeps your own agent away from your mail; from H1 an instruction does, checked by its tool-call record (H2, H3, I3), until the house-rules plugin's coded check exists in wave C, which the catalog's test T5 must first show can be written. Between the two parents nothing changes: Jordan's agent holds no Gmail tool, denies yours by name and has no hand-off. The morning text is not affected either, because the scheduler starts the reader directly.

None of this has run. That the reader keeps its tools when started this way is read from the installed code; that its answer comes back into the same chat is the catalog's test T4. If T4 or the seeded-mail test fails, the lookup is switched off again and moves to wave B. H5, the reply check, and H6, the record, are done either way. Do only H5 and H6, and leave the lookup to wave B, in three cases: you chose that at A4; C3 showed a doubled job; or the plan is over its hours when the morning text and the sign-in check are live.
