Self-hosted family assistant · planning set

Happy

A personal assistant for one household. It runs on a Mac mini at home under its own macOS account, and the family talks to it by iMessage. These pages are the plans: the architecture, the roadmap, a step-by-step runbook for each milestone with its execution log, and the catalog of what Happy should learn next.
